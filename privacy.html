Privacy policy

Last updated 14 October 2026

Claude Mail Bridge is a personal tool that connects the Claude AI assistant to your Google accounts. This policy explains what it does with your data.

What it accesses

When you sign in with Google, the app asks for access to Gmail, Google Drive and Google Sheets (and may also request Calendar, Contacts, Docs, Tasks, Search Console and Meet). It uses that access to read, search, label, archive, move to Trash, draft and send email, and to read, create and edit Drive files and spreadsheets, only when you ask the assistant to.

Where your data goes

The app runs on your own computer and has no server of its own. When you ask Claude to work with your email or files, the relevant content is sent to Anthropic's Claude service so the assistant can process your request. Anthropic handles that content under its own terms and privacy policy. The app does not send your data to the developer or anyone else. It does not sell, share or use your data for advertising, and it does not use your data to train AI models.

Your sign-in tokens are stored encrypted on your own computer.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Claude Mail Bridge's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's configuration folder deletes its stored tokens.
